How these numbers were derived

One lead file per show, per year. Every show folder in the archive holds a master lead list plus a copy for each sales rep containing only that rep's follow-up assignments. Only the master file is counted — per-rep copies, daily partial downloads, mailing-label extracts and archived drafts are all excluded. The exact file used is named under "Source" in each year's drill-down.

From 2017 onward, the lead count is Hot + Warm only. Every workbook was reviewed using its own schema. Staff-entered Hot/Warm fields are authoritative; legacy Application/Potential Application, iCapture questions, and distributor A/B grades are mapped to Hot/Warm. When no usable classification exists, the application notes are reviewed individually: concrete active applications are Hot, plausible future applications are Warm, and general interest, vendors, internal/test scans, blanks, and "no application" records are excluded. Cold is the residual of valid source rows minus Hot and Warm. Earlier ungraded files remain unchanged.

Costs are what the archive documents. Where the show folder holds the company's own expense report ("… Expense Breakdown / Estimated Expenses / Final Expenses") those itemised figures are used, preferring a final/actual report over an estimate — these cover most shows through about 2014. Otherwise the total is built from the invoices, POs and order confirmations in the folder, grouped into booth space, furniture & decor, electrical, lead retrieval, freight/drayage, labour, registration, AV/internet and the rest.

Figures are only taken from an explicit total ("Total", "Amount due", "Gesamtbetrag" …); price lists, sponsorship menus, attendance counts, declared shipping values and insurance limits are filtered out. The same charge documented twice — an order confirmation and its receipt, a deposit and its parent invoice — is counted once, and because booth space and lead retrieval are a single charge per show, the largest documented figure is taken rather than the sum of its instalments. Non-USD invoices are converted at a fixed reference rate.

A cost of "—" means no cost document was found for that year, not that the show was free. A "~" prefix marks a figure known to be incomplete — typically peripheral charges with no booth invoice on file — and those years are left out of cost-per-lead so the comparison stays like-for-like. Cost coverage is thinnest for the European shows (Hannover, Analytica, Compamed), whose invoices were largely handled outside these folders.

Where a show folder holds no lead export, the reference workbook is used instead. Tradeshow report data.xlsx is the marketing team's own Hot + Warm summary for 2020–2025. Eight show-years are counted from it because their folders hold a lead-retrieval order but no exported lead list: MD&M West, MD&M Minneapolis, Hannover, Analytica, BIOMEDevice Silicon Valley and Compamed for 2024, and A&D Turin and FEINDEF for 2025. Those rows name the workbook under "Source" and give a combined Hot + Warm figure with no Hot/Warm split, because that is all the workbook records. Wherever a show folder does hold a lead file, that file is counted and the workbook is not used — each such year's note states whether the workbook agrees.

Gaps are shown as gaps. Years with no leads carry a status — cancelled, declined, walked (attended without a booth), or no-data — and a note saying what the archive does and does not hold for that year. A cancelled show whose charges were refunded carries a documented cost of $0 with the refund correspondence cited, which is different from a blank. Nothing on this page is estimated or interpolated: every lead count and every cost line traces back to a named file — the show folder's own documents, or the reference workbook where it is the only record.
