How these numbers were derived

One lead file per show, per year. Every show folder in the archive holds a master lead list plus a copy for each sales rep containing only that rep's follow-up assignments. Only the master file is counted — per-rep copies, daily partial downloads, mailing-label extracts and archived drafts are all excluded. The exact file used is named under "Source" in each year's drill-down.

Cold leads are excluded. Where the master file carries a lead rating (Lead Rank, Lead Ranking, Product Interest Level, Lead Interest, iCapture's Q n: Lead Ranking, or the free-text "Hot/Warm/Cold?" survey field), rows marked Cold are removed from the lead count. Hot, Warm and unrated rows are kept. Ratings appear in most shows from 2017–2018 onward; earlier files carry no rating, so every row counts. Each year's drill-down shows the full mix and how many cold rows were dropped.

Costs are what the archive documents. Where the show folder holds the company's own expense report ("… Expense Breakdown / Estimated Expenses / Final Expenses") those itemised figures are used, preferring a final/actual report over an estimate — these cover most shows through about 2014. Otherwise the total is built from the invoices, POs and order confirmations in the folder, grouped into booth space, furniture & decor, electrical, lead retrieval, freight/drayage, labour, registration, AV/internet and the rest.

Figures are only taken from an explicit total ("Total", "Amount due", "Gesamtbetrag" …); price lists, sponsorship menus, attendance counts, declared shipping values and insurance limits are filtered out. The same charge documented twice — an order confirmation and its receipt, a deposit and its parent invoice — is counted once, and because booth space and lead retrieval are a single charge per show, the largest documented figure is taken rather than the sum of its instalments. Non-USD invoices are converted at a fixed reference rate.

A cost of "—" means no cost document was found for that year, not that the show was free. A "~" prefix marks a figure known to be incomplete — typically peripheral charges with no booth invoice on file — and those years are left out of cost-per-lead so the comparison stays like-for-like. Cost coverage is thinnest for the European shows (Hannover, Analytica, Compamed), whose invoices were largely handled outside these folders.

Gaps are shown as gaps. Years with no leads carry a status — cancelled, declined, walked (attended without a booth), or no-data — and a note saying what the archive does and does not hold for that year. Nothing on this page is estimated or interpolated: every lead count and every cost line traces back to a named file in the show folder.
