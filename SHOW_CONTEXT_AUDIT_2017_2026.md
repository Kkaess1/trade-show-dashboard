# Trade-show differentiator audit, 2017–2026

The dashboard’s show detail view now includes source-backed context intended to help compare why one show-year may have performed differently from another. All 61 show-years with lead data from 2017 onward have a synopsis. Two additional Space Tech Expo years are included because BSE used a speaker/workshop format in 2023 and has a post-show market report for 2026 despite having no saved lead export.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Completed BSE post-show and trip reports
2. BSE pre-show overviews, campaigns and show plans
3. Internal correspondence documenting an activity or decision
4. Organizer post-show reports, only when they provide relevant audience or format context
5. An explicit archive-gap note when no document supports a differentiating claim

Marketing solicitations and lists of opportunities offered by an organizer were not treated as activities BSE performed. Planned items are described as planned, cancelled items as cancelled, and archive gaps are not filled by inference.

## Included factors

- Speaking, workshops, panels and conference participation
- Product launches, demos, video and other booth content
- Customer appointments, dinners, partner meetings and canvassing
- Pre-show outreach, editorial activity and customer invitations
- Booth location, booth redesigns and notable staffing changes
- Attendance, traffic, competition and other documented market conditions
- Distributor or sister-company participation

These factors are presented as possible performance context, not proof that an activity caused a particular lead result. Each dashboard synopsis displays its source filename so the underlying report can be checked in the trade-show archive.
